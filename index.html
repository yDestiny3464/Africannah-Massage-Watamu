Where can I get a massage in Watamu?

How do I book?

Tap any “Book on WhatsApp” button, choose your massage and a time, and we will confirm.

How much is a massage?

Prices depend on the treatment. Message us on WhatsApp for current prices and promotions.

Is prenatal massage safe?

It is designed for expecting mothers, but please check with your doctor or midwife first and tell us how far along you are when booking.

Deep tissue or relaxation, which should I choose?

Choose deep tissue for tight muscles and knots. Choose fresh & relax if you want to unwind. Not sure? Ask us on WhatsApp.

What is reflexology good for?

It uses pressure on points of the feet and hands. Many people find it very relaxing and a lovely treat for tired feet.
